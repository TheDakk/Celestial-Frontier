# 1. Earth Creatures / Fauna

## Verdict
**Gold-ready.**

This was one of the last categories carrying visible review debt. It now clears the bar.

## What improved successfully

### Deferred fauna fixes
The prior four named holdouts are now visibly addressed:

- **Eagle**: the brow ledge and more raptor-like stance improve the read.
- **Gerenuk**: the neck length is now materially more distinct from the neighboring antelope family.
- **Red Panda**: the ringed tail now reads clearly.
- **Poison Dart Frog**: the aposematic pattern survives much better at small size.

### Overall family status

- frogs now separate clearly enough as a family set
- turtle/tortoise/snapping-turtle distinction is stable
- birds still stylize heavily, but are now readable within the project’s language
- ungulates have adequate family separation
- small mammals are no longer collapsing into one generic form

## Remaining notes

These are **polish notes only**, not blockers:

- the eagle could still become slightly more iconic with an even stronger hooked beak and chest mass
- the poison dart frog is much better, but still depends partly on contrast rather than shape
- some bird families remain intentionally simplified, so realism-driven viewers may still want more species-specific silhouette spread

# 3. Procedural Creatures and Procedural Flora

## Verdict
**Gold-ready.**

This is the biggest conceptual win compared with earlier packages.

## What is working now

### Surface integration
The earlier “painted-on” problem is no longer a major review issue.

The **ENGRAVE v2** direction in `proc-skins.png` is the right answer:

- materials now read as relief in the creature’s own body
- the treatment covers the full body rather than only center patches
- it stays inside the silhouette
- chitin, scales, feathers, slick/wet, plating, translucency, and crystalline types all read more naturally

### Wings
`winged.png` now reads correctly as an anatomical system:

- grounded forms sit low and feel weight-bearing
- airborne forms lift and use narrowed shadows
- wing roots no longer look pasted on
- front/back wing layering reads properly

### General system quality

- body plans remain strong
- heads and features are readable
- swarm sheet still works well
- aquatic conversions remain excellent
- procedural flora forms feel coherent and no longer obviously placeholder-like

## Remaining notes

Small polish notes only:

- fur is improved, though it is still the least dimensional of the surface classes
- crystalline forms remain a little understated compared with how strong the feathered/scaled pass now is

These are not blockers.
