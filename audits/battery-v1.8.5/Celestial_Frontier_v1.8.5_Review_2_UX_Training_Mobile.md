# Celestial Frontier v1.8.5 — Review 2: UX, Training, Mobile, and Casual Player

## Score: 9.0/10

## Verdict

The first-session experience is clearer and more responsive than prior builds. Training completed across phone, tablet, and desktop under both normal and aggressive input.

## What worked

- 8/8 complete real-click training sessions
- 0 stalls
- 0 modal-stack breaks
- 0 page or console errors
- Nine responsive viewport checks found no horizontal overflow
- Touch controls measured at least 44×44 on phone/tablet
- Search/name inputs are 16px on mobile
- Compendium and Fabricator start tidy and collapsed
- The objective chip unfolds into a useful live quest log
- Charter progress updates without reopening the panel
- Denials explain what is missing and provide a working destination button
- Stall guidance suggests a specific available action rather than repeating the goal
- First click feedback is effectively immediate

## Friction

The stall helper triggers after roughly ten interactions. That is useful for a confused player but may become nagging for someone browsing deliberately. Trigger it from repeated failed/no-progress actions and elapsed time, not a flat interaction count alone.

The specimen card’s XP sentence is outdated and still tells players only victories grant XP. That hides one of the best v1.8 improvements.

## Casual-player outlook

The build now does a better job answering:

- What am I trying to do?
- Why can’t I do this?
- What am I missing?
- Where should I go next?

That is a major usability improvement.
