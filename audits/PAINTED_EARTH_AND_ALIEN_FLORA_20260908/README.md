## Concrete visual brief

**Earth:** the sheet combines recognizable complete plant growth forms with naturalistic animal
anatomy. Broadleaf crowns, conifer tiers, branching berry shrubs, palm fronds, grasses, fern
fronds and flowers have different structures and material detail. Fauna use coherent bodies,
natural coat/feather/shell/scale colors, readable faces and selective directional texture.
Strong lit edges and darker internal overlaps give volume without changing the organism's
silhouette. Carry this richness into each existing named Earth species: preserve its proportions,
markings and botanical leaf/flower/fruit identity. The unlabelled examples do not assign catalog
species or authorize copying a generic berry shrub onto Cranberry, Persimmon or Devil's Club.

**Alien flora:** the twelve examples vary the entire growth architecture: ribbed fans, branching
clusters, inflated pods, layered rosettes, translucent veined bulbs, intertwined bushes, long
curling blades, perforated trunks, porous coral-like fans and split seed cases. Detail connects
the base, trunk, branches and terminal structures into one organism. Carry this whole-form
variation and coherent material transitions into compatible seeded families; retain deterministic
identity, biome mapping and palette. Orange, blue, ivory and dark bark demonstrate contrast
options, not a universal recolor. The display-sheet roots demonstrate attachment and branching;
in-world exposed roots depend on the organism and ground context, and are not mandatory.

Shared priorities are coherent whole-body or whole-plant volume, readable silhouette, local
material texture and selective highlights. Preserve appropriate movement: attached foliage
bends with its supporting structure, and Earth animals retain species-appropriate motion.
These sheets do not supply hidden anatomy, motion clips, metre-scale proportions or new ecology.
