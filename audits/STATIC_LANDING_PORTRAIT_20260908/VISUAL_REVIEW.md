# Static landing — native visual review

September 8, 2026. Codex inspected the full PNG captures returned by the isolated
Edge observer at desktop 1440×1000 DPR1 and phone 390×844 DPR2. This is an agent
visual assessment, not Nick's human acceptance or physical iPhone/Safari review.

The Civet and riverbank read as one opaque painting. Its paws meet the stony bank;
fur, wet stones, trees and distant hills share the painting's atmosphere. There
is no extracted-alpha fringe, checkerboard or separate resident plane to expose.
The complete composition fits above the existing Biosphere strip and preserves
the current controls. The scene remains a rectangular painting within the
accepted game layout; it is not a newly approved edge-to-edge layout.

The native captures preserve clear fur, foliage and river detail at their tested
display sizes. The original 1875×839 master is untouched. The 960×430 runtime tier
is smaller; this review does not qualify enlarged/4K/other-DPR viewing or claim
that downsampling retains all master detail. Lossless WebP adds no RGB changes
after the documented resize.
