# Mussel source correction basis

Read-only source research, 2026-10-02. Existing painting: `audits/C196_SIMPLE_ORIGINALS_20261002/04-mussel/master.png`; prior hold remains immutable. The source correction targets a marine blue-mussel form while retaining canonical Mussel identity.

Naturalis describes the anterior beaks, the long mostly concealed dorsal ligament, a posterior mantle margin forming a short imperfect siphon, and ventral byssus near the umbones. These observations support correcting the earlier oversized posterior tongue-like projection and exposing a modest posterior aperture; they do not authorize inventing long clam-like tubes. [Naturalis species description](https://ns-mollusca.linnaeus.naturalis.nl/linnaeus_ng/app/views/species/taxon.php?id=121661).

The primary paper on mussel byssus describes threads arising from a stem at the foot base. We retain threads as anchoring tissue, never as additional feet. [Benedict and Waite,1986, PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3772980/).

This is a painting request, not proof that the generated anatomy is correct. A full-size observation and ordinary source-bound intake must decide whether hinge, mantle, foot and aperture can actually be authored. Hidden present structures will not be marked absent to pass the current inventory. Plated shell/hinge and slick visible soft tissue remain the existing closed material categories. No runtime change.
