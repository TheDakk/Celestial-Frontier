# Painted Civet visual review — 2026-09-08

Agent visual assessment, not Nick/Claude human acceptance. The root and independent review agent
inspected the retained source, matte previews and reference sheets. The root also inspected the
first native 440 px rest portrait and exact 960×430 Earth inset; final evidence is identified in
`final-results.json` when present. Numeric checks do not substitute for these visual observations.

The new treatment retains one connected Civet body, a long pointed muzzle, rounded ears, four
distinct planted feet, tan spotted coat, dark mask and ringed tail. It adds directional fur,
layered shading and visible material volume. The torso and hindquarters look fuller than the
flat baseline, while the recognizable species form and posture remain. Its rendering is much
closer to the supplied Earth flora/fauna sheet; no fox conversion or style restart is warranted.
This is comparative agent judgment, not an exact anatomical/pixel identity guarantee.

The recovered alpha is approximate. There is a pale fringe on the back and underside at high
magnification, interrupted whiskers and small pale fragments around the mouth/nose; the nose
edge can look patchy on light backing. Source RGB byte preservation does not prove alpha quality.
These issues remain visible in retained close matte previews and require production edge review.
The current asset is appropriate for this bounded motion study, not declared production clean.

In the Earth inset the Civet is clearly richer than the five retained residents. Its existing
relative anchor and whole scene background remain fixed. Persimmon, Cranberry, Devil's Club,
Frog and Platypus still expose the older flat art, especially the sparse tree and angular flora;
the new references do not make those assets finished. Botanical replacements must preserve each
named species' actual growth form and fruit/leaf identity, not inherit a generic sheet example.
Scene scale is the approved relative study placement, not measured real-world ecology.

An independent reviewer inspected the 440/132 strike, breathe and Earth frames: connected
anatomy remains readable, with no visible limb or neck separation in those samples. Fur compresses
to mottled detail at 132 px. The Earth placement does not clip or collide with the adjacent plant,
but the warm bright coat and weak contact shadow still look layered onto the wet bank. Root
inspected the final full desktop and phone documents and their scene/portrait arrangement.
Stills do not establish timing; the separate native finite-motion result measures that boundary.

The single painted view limits motion to subtle breathing and connected neck brace/thrust/recoil.
No walking, jaw action, turning animal, unseen anatomy or native ability-specific animation is
claimed. Native Compendium/Chronicle/Planetside integration and final human art acceptance remain
separate future work. All earlier verification and campaign limits are retained in ROADMAP.md.
