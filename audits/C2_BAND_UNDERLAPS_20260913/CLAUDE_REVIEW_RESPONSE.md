## 1. Implementation against the authorization

- Depth formula: `bandDepth` is pivot distance × sin(sum of joint limit magnitudes, saturated at 90°) × 1.1, floor 0.02 W, cap W/8, at every ancestor-owned boundary pixel with exact Euclidean discs stamping only the descendant's opaque ink. **As specified.**
- Attachment and ordering: bands attach to the ancestor joint, are grouped by ancestor joint and descendant layer, and draw beneath base parts in the descendant's layer through the unchanged joint-patch representation. **As specified.** Grouping merges cuts that share an ancestor and layer; that loses nothing at runtime because all of a group's pixels move with the same joint.
- Rest: 0 changed channels for disc-only and bands. **Confirmed.** Poses: the recoil, strike and both authored stride extrema mapped through the actual phase and clip durations; the disc-only control is retained on every frame. **Confirmed.**
- **One implementation defect, and it is the big one.** `cutChain` walks the joint graph from the descendant's declared joint to the ancestor's declared joint. The torso part is declared as `spine`, but the hips (`hindFarRoot`, `hindNearRoot`) and `tail0` hang from `pelvis`, which is `spine`'s parent, so `Knee → Root → pelvis → root` never meets `spine` and the walk returns null. That is why the declaration reports the twelve "non-ancestor adjacencies": `hind-far-upper|torso`, `hind-near-upper|torso`, `hind-far-lower|torso`, `hind-near-lower|torso`, `tail-root|torso`, `tail-middle|torso`, `chest|hind-far-upper` and the leg-to-leg and leg-to-neck pairs. The first six are not siblings in the painted anatomy: the torso remainder owns the pelvis, both hips and `tail0`, so it is the ancestor of every hind leg and of the tail. The record's joint graph is right; the walk must resolve a joint that has no part of its own (`root`, `pelvis`, the four `*Root`, `tail0`, the two `ear*Root`) to the part that owns its pixels: the part whose declared joint it is, else the owner of its parent joint, and for `pelvis`/`root` the remainder part. With that map the torso becomes the ancestor of the hind uppers (chain `Knee, Root`) and of `tail-root` (chain `tail1, tail0`), and those six cuts get bands. The remaining six (`fore-far-upper|fore-near-upper`, `fore-far-upper|neck`, `fore-near-upper|neck`, `hind-far-upper|hind-near-upper`, `hind-near-upper|tail-root`, `chest|hind-far-upper`) are true siblings that only touch because the painting overlaps them; they need no band and should stay listed.

Which band leaks cannot be read off the composite render; it needs the pair-isolated gate below.
- **Ears: worse (3,010 → 4,867).** The zoom shows a second, whole ear behind the far ear: the band copied the entire ear. `bandDepth` at an ear-base boundary pixel is its distance to the ear pivot × sin(saturated 90°) × 1.1, so the disc radius is larger than the ear itself and the "band" is the whole appendage. When the ear swings the static copy stays: a ghost, which the oracle counts as enclosed transparent pixels between the copy and the moved ear. Same cause for the tail tip and the paws (bands of 25 to 133 px on parts 40 to 80 px thick). The rule must be capped by the descendant's own size.
- **Legs: unchanged or worse (fore-near ankle 14,598 → 10,935, hind-far ankle 16,670 → 17,441).** Two separate things. The hip cuts had no bands at all (the defect in section 1), so the visible hip wedges in the ankle zoom are expected. On top of that the oracle's 10 percent discs at the ankles enclose the space *between* the far and near legs, which is legitimately background and narrower than the 4 percent closing when the legs swing; those thousands are mostly not seams. The disc oracle is honest but blind there.
- **Torso/chest and approach**: chest improved everywhere; the approach extrema improved at head, neck and knees for the same ancestry reason and stayed bad where the hips have no band.

## 3. Ruler clarification (not applied to this failed gate)

The composite-render oracle over-counts wherever two limbs run parallel or an appendage lifts off its base. The gate should be **pair-isolated**: render, at the same pose, only the ancestor base part, its band(s), and the descendant base part, transparent elsewhere, and run `seam-oracle.mjs` on that render with `--disc=0.06` (75 px) around the descendant's pivot. A pair render cannot contain another limb's pocket, and a lifted ear's notch beyond the disc is not counted. Pose-minus-rest must be 0 per pair; the disc-only declaration per pair is the failing control. This gate FAILED under the composite ruler and stays recorded as failed.
