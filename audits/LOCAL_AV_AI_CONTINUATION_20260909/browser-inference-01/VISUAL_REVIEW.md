## Visible strengths

The output reads as one coherent wet riverbank scene. Cool overcast illumination,
shared green/gray reflected light, mist-softened mountains and forest, water
reflections, and mossy foreground rocks belong together. The animals sit on
plausible surfaces: the Civet's feet meet its bank, the Platypus meets the left
water edge, and the Frog rests on a foreground rock. Local dark contact and
vegetation overlaps avoid the detached cutout appearance of earlier scene studies.
No obvious alpha fringe or pasted rectangular boundary is visible in this opaque
painting. Foreground detail and softer distant contrast give convincing depth.

All six intended subject categories can be located without labels. Their broad
composition follows the supplied Earth painting closely, including the upper-left
fruit tree, right-hand palmate foliage and red clusters, and lower-left berry patch.
This similarity demonstrates useful reference influence for this one scene; it
does not demonstrate diverse seed/world generation or exact individual identity.

## Painting detail and acceptance boundary

At its native768×432 size the image is attractive and legible, with substantial
water, moss, foliage and fur texture. The approved1672×941 source holds visibly
more fine structure. The generated painting compresses animal facial detail,
small leaves, paws and botany; some surface texture reads as coarser stippling or
repetition. Dense rain streaks are also more visually explicit. Broad scene
cohesion is closer to the target than exact character/botanical fidelity.

This inspection does not establish equality with the approved painting at larger
landing display sizes or high DPR. It does not qualify a Compendium cutout,
articulated battle asset, all19 roster identities, other biomes, a phone runtime,
or production delivery. Preserve this result as the first successful browser
inference. The next bounded quality proof, if commissioned, should hold the scene
recipe explicit while supplying the selected Civet master and assessing the same
facial/coat features; no new run was undertaken in this review.
