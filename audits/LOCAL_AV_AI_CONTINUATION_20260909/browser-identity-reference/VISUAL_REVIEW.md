## What improved

The new Civet has a longer pale pointed muzzle, a clearer dark lateral face mask, a visible
warm amber eye and a lighter golden tawny coat with scattered dark spots. These are visibly
closer to the selected Civet master than the first run's gray, shorter-looking face and dense
dark coat pattern. The head now reads in a clearer side view, making the muzzle and mask
easier to compare. Rounded ears, a substantial torso, short legs and the long banded tail
remain legible. This is a meaningful directional gain, not exact matching of all markings.

The riverbank arrangement remains coherent: the animals occupy plausible moss/rock/water-edge
surfaces, foreground overlaps and contact shadows ground them, and water reflections, wet
rocks, forest and mist share a consistent environment. There is no visible pasted rectangle
or obvious alpha halo around the generated Civet. The additional identity reference did not
bring its neutral matte or cutout fringe into the visible scene. All six intended broad
subject categories remain locatable without labels.

The recipes retain the same model revision, output size, seed and four steps, but this run
adds both the Civet reference and explicit identity instructions. The improvement therefore
supports the combined conditioning approach; it does not isolate image conditioning from
prompt changes or establish repeated cross-seed identity fidelity.

## Detail and next bounded quality experiment

At native 768×432, this is a pleasing, readable scene. Relative to the approved 1672×941 painting,
fur, small leaves, paw/toe edges and diagnostic plant structures still have less resolved detail;
some texture is coarse or repetitive. More pixels in the approved reference are part of that
comparison, so this review does not attribute the entire gap to the model. Neither upscaling
this output nor technical inference success establishes the target's full-size finish.

The likely next focused experiment is **one native 1024×576 output of this same two-reference
recipe**, keeping the model/runtime, seed, four steps, prompt and input references fixed.
Compare whether the extra native pixel budget improves Civet fur and the small botanical and
animal features, while retaining scene cohesion; record the added latency/memory separately.
Do not retouch or upscale the result to conceal limitations. This tests resolution/detail,
not a guarantee of corrected exact markings, tail framing or universal organism generation.
