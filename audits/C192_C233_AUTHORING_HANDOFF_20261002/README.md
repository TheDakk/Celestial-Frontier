# C233 authoring refusals: material decisions and successor routing

The C233 summary has seven material refusals. A whole-profile mapping is unsafe: the canonical xenarthran profile includes Sloth and Armadillo (Pangolin has a separate toothless-clawed-mammal profile), and freshwater-turtle includes both scuted shells and Softshell Turtle. Use named-source decisions before a generic profile mapping; retain refusal for unmatched names. These are existing closed Motion Kit categories, not new material laws.

| C233 subject | Closed body category | Remaining scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sloth | furred (source spelling fur) | The coarse coat supports this category. It does not attest contacts, claws or hidden limbs. Do not map every xenarthran to fur. |
| Tortoise, Box Turtle | plated | Plated selects the existing rigid secondary-material rule; observed exposed neck/limb/tail chains need their own scaled overrides where authored. This does not prove rigid shell geometry. |
| Pond Turtle, Snapping Turtle, Turtle | plated | Same proposed shell rule; do not turn a shell category into rigid exposed neck/limb/tail chains. Named sources must show the claimed surface. |
| Softshell Turtle | HELD for observed shell/skin partition | The shell has leathery covering rather than scutes. Neither a blanket freshwater-turtle=plated nor whole-body slick decision describes this mixed source. Keep its framing hold too. |

The kit already supports record.materials.joints and rejects unknown joint names/materials. The base surface initializes every joint, including the tail. Shell and exposed-chain partition must be authored against the real source before any joint override is supplied. No anatomy is invented here and no fit/native acceptance is claimed. This audit-only handoff does not modify the scoring runner or the certified product.

Biology sources reviewed 2026-10-02: [Smithsonian two-toed sloth](https://nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/two-toed-sloth) describes the coarse coat; [Smithsonian tortoise shell](https://nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/news/meet-our-cool-creep-aldabra-tortoises) identifies shell as skeleton; [University of Florida turtle anatomy](https://ufdcimages.uflib.ufl.edu/IR/00/00/24/33/00001/UW15900.pdf) distinguishes scutes from the softshell leathery covering. These establish source anatomy, while the closed motion category choice above is an engineering interpretation.

Other C233 routes: Impala/Dog/Wolverine/Badger/Beaver have new source paintings in C192_CREATURE_SUPPLY; Serval has a separate source-hold authoring queue. Banana Slug awaits the now-reviewed D31 product plus references; Prawn/Shrimp/Snail/Clam/Mussel reference work is in C192_SPECIALIZED_REFERENCES. Crab and Brittle Star were accepted in C188; Wolf, Jaguar and Otter are already accepted and must not be replaced by stale refusals. Seahorse root and the Giant Squid/Vampire Squid/Nautilus facing or eye refusals remain truthful source-authoring holds; do not mirror or invent eyes to bypass them.

Independent review in C192_C233_MATERIAL_REVIEW corrected profile scope and exposed-tail requirements. Each row now binds its exact C233 source master and stays machine-readably held until its required authoring is complete. Material selection alone does not prove shell deformation, anatomy, fit, or motion admission.
