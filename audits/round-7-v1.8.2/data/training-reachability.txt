TRAINING REACHABILITY — CF1802-01
Walks the real 21-step Field Training on each viewport. At every step, for every visible surface,
samples a 7x9 grid inside its on-screen rect and asks document.elementFromPoint who wins the hit
test. "reachable = N%" is the fraction of sample points where the element (or a descendant) is
topmost. 0% means nothing inside it can be pressed.
Harness: harness/tutreach3.mjs  (surface-level data: data/tutreach.json, from tutreach2.mjs)
