# C183 independent-source review — 2026-10-02

These are full-size observations of the five new C213 paintings. New canonical subjects establish source independence; they do not establish anatomical completeness, reference admission or moving-paint quality. No source pixels, template inventory, material law or D28 cap are changed.

| Subject | Concrete observation | Authoring outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leech | Complete curved body and both terminal sucker rims. The pronounced deep funnel cavities and glossy cylindrical body need biological/style review. | Independent centreline authoring drafted. Twelve curve controls are not twelve biological segments; no invented eyes, legs or hidden mouth apparatus. |
| Sea Squirt | Both siphons and the continuous anchored tunic are visible. The internal basket lattice is unusually prominent and the basal tissue resembles roots. | Independent coarse authoring drafted. The existing aperture controls the larger top siphon; the lateral siphon remains in the crown. Independent two-siphon animation is unavailable in this template. |
| Banana Slug | Both long ocular tentacles, both shorter sensory tentacles, mantle, pneumostome and continuous foot are visible. The mouth position is only an ambiguous small crease beside the short tentacles. | Anatomical observation hold. The template requires a mouth landmark, which is not fabricated from the ambiguous crease. Shell absence is biologically supported, but does not resolve the mouth. |
| Prawn | Abdomen, tail fan, rostrum, eyes, long antennae, antennules and ventral swimmerets are retained. Far thoracic roots overlap; ten complete walking chains cannot be traced. | Anatomical observation hold. No hidden far-root coordinates, invented walking limbs, deletion of swimmerets or conversion of swimmerets into walking legs. |
| Crab | Both open claws, their fingers and both eye stalks are visible. Several posterior walking chains disappear behind the carapace or other legs. | Anatomical observation hold. Eight independent walking chains cannot be proved; no fabricated hidden pair or biological-absence declaration. |

The three held sources need painting-side clarification: expose Banana Slug's actual oral opening without adding a false proboscis; separate Prawn's five genuine walking-leg pairs at their roots while retaining all swimmerets and head appendages; separate Crab's four genuine walking-leg pairs through each visible knee and tip. Existing originals remain unchanged. The slugs' fine sensory pair and Prawn's swimmerets must remain source paint even though their current coarse templates have no dedicated controls.

The Leech and Sea Squirt drafts still require exact retention, intake, static, conservation and full-size ownership review. They are not registered. Validation is deferred during the C216 I5/develop isolation window and will run only in the explicitly announced offline slot.
