FROZEN STYLE
  Classic painted science-fantasy oil illustration in the hand of the TSR
  masters Gerald Brom, Keith Parkinson and Jeff Easley, carried off-world:
  painterly heroic realism with visible confident oil brushwork, rich colour
  deepened by dramatic warm-versus-cool lighting and strong chiaroscuro,
  grounded weighty anatomy and hard-used equipment, crisp ornate detail at the
  focal point softening toward the edges, with Brom's macabre elegance
  surfacing in the strange and predatory subjects. The world is a frontier of
  unmapped planets: sun-scoured hull plate, scored ceramic, woven fibre and
  patinated alloy in place of mail and plate, salvage and field repair on every
  surface; alien life built of chitin, membrane, mineral growth and
  bioluminescent pigment rather than fur and feather; each world lit by one
  distinct colour of sun that falls across the whole subject. Serious and
  wondrous in tone, never cartoonish, never photorealistic, never anime, never
  cel-shaded, never a 3D render. Museum-quality oil illustration, extremely
  high detail, sharp focus.

SYSTEM CARD
SYSTEM CARD - Amber Glass (reference study; no new game system)
  Star: AMBER, an orange sun. Pigment: burnt orange, gold, ochre.
  Light: warm, honeyed, medium contrast; from upper left, long shadows toward
    lower right, dark olive-brown shadows.
  Mineral palette: glossy black glass, matte ochre sand, rust-red stone.
  Atmosphere: thin dust-orange haze, washing distant forms toward pale ochre.
  Flora pigment: ochre, rust, olive-black.
  Fauna adaptation: banded rust and tan camouflage against ochre and olive-black
    growth, medium-sized shaded eyes, ridged chitin shedding daytime heat.
  One signature: black glass dunes.

SUBJECT
One square reference sheet: about twelve subjects per row, rows for fauna,
flora, people, ships and items, plus one orbital planet. Twelve per row holds;
sixteen wraps. All subjects belong to the one system card above, painted by
one hand. Each subject is separate and complete, never touching another.
Fauna row: twelve distinct alien organisms, banded rust and tan matte chitin,
olive-black membrane and black glass mineral growth; weighty quadrupeds,
six-legged crawlers and winged membrane creatures, no armour or harness.
Quadrupeds each have four legs, two near and two far, one head, one head only;
six-legged crawlers each have six legs, three near and three far, one head,
one head only; fliers each have two membrane wings, one on each side, two
legs, one on each side, one head, one head only. Faces have paired shaded
medium eyes, two eyes, a ridged brow and closed jaws, watchful or predatory.
Flora row: twelve separate growth forms: stalks, canopies, mineral corals,
seed forms; matte ochre, waxy rust and olive-black membrane, whole bases visible.
People row: twelve separate frontier explorers and traders, one head, two
arms, two hands, two legs each; one head, an arm and hand on each side, a leg
on each side. Scored ochre ceramic over olive-black woven fibre, patinated
alloy and rust-red field repairs; faces visible, appraising or resolute.
Ships row: twelve separate compact vessels, glossy black glass fittings,
scored ochre hull ceramic and rust-red patch plates, drives as structure;
three-quarter from slightly above, noses to lower left, every hull complete.
Items row: twelve separate tools, salvage cells, eggs and relics in black
glass, ochre ceramic and patinated alloy, readable at small size.
One orbital planet below: one whole disc, one disc only; black glass dune
bands and ochre land, thin dust-orange atmosphere contained inside its opaque
edge; no moons, zero moons, no rings, zero rings. Upper-left lit limb.

ACCURACY
Five rows of about twelve subjects, then one orbital planet. Every figure,
growth, vessel and object is individually isolated on the magenta key. Full
anatomy and functional parts inside each subject's clear margin. No scenery,
floor, cast shadow or ground base. No cropped bodies or overlapping subjects.

LAYOUT
One square sheet, rows for fauna, flora, people, ships and items, plus one
orbital planet. Approximately twelve per row, not sixteen. Unlabelled.
Fauna and people face front-to-three-quarter. Flora shows complete base to
crown. Ships in three-quarter view from above. Items on a readable diagonal.
This section 9b reference-sheet layout is the explicit exception to the
production contact-sheet ban in the unchanged shared negative below.

TECHNICAL OUTPUT
  Create a square 1024 x 1024 PNG. Every pixel that is not the subject is one
  flat, uniform, pure magenta fill (#FF00FF): no gradient, vignette, texture,
  noise, checkerboard or transparency pattern, and no ground, base, frame,
  scenery or cast shadow. The subject's outline is crisp and fully opaque
  against the magenta, with no glow, haze, smoke or soft feathering bleeding
  into it, and nothing on the subject is magenta or pink. The magenta is keyed
  out afterwards to produce the transparent asset.

NEGATIVE
  No text, letters, numbers, watermark, signature, border, frame, grid, UI
  element, health bar, minimap, waypoint, token ring, base, pedestal,
  photorealism, anime, chibi styling, cel shading, 3D render, plastic toy
  sheen, airbrushed gradients, duplicate subject, unintended companion, extra
  limbs, missing limbs, malformed hands, cropped anatomy, or contact-sheet
  layout. No lens flare, no chromatic aberration, no holographic UI
  projections, no glowing outlines or rim light used as an effect.
  No scenery, no floor plane, no cast shadow, no drop shadow, no white
  background, no checkered background, no baked transparency pattern.
  Background must be one flat uniform magenta fill and nothing else.
