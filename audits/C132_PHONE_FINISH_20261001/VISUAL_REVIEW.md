# SDXS native-mac-01 visual review — rejected

All five originals, reconstructions and finished PNGs were inspected at their original 1254×1254 dimensions. **All five finishes are visually rejected.** The retained Mac result is a valid 5/5 conservation pass, not an acceptable creature finisher. No output is admitted into the game or accepted library.

| Subject | Reconstruction | Finished output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finch | Recognizable silhouette and broad plumage, but feather detail and the eye are softened; green/purple contamination appears along head, beak, tail and feet. | Feather groups, the eye and beak interior are replaced by brown/teal scene-like patches. Narrow protected strips expose a mosaic of part boundaries. Rejected. |
| Sandpiper | Recognizable long beak and leg arrangement, but the eye ring, feather markings and small toes degrade; green/purple contour and leg coloration is introduced. | Eye and most spotted/striped plumage disappear into unrelated soft brown/teal patches. The retained edge strips contrast sharply with the replaced interior. Rejected. |
| Wasp | Recognizable body plan, but fine wing veins, antenna/leg articulation and surface detail are blurred; green/orange color shifts replace parts of the original black/yellow material. | Black/yellow abdominal bands, thorax markings, wing membrane/veins and eye are overwritten by unrelated interior patches. Thin original structures surviving the mask do not rescue the creature. Rejected. |
| Ptarmigan | Broad white form remains, but feather layering, toe detail and eye clarity degrade; head, tail and feet acquire colored edge artifacts. | White plumage becomes brown/teal scene-like surfaces, with bright white protected part boundaries outlining the replacement. The head and feathered legs lose their material identity. Rejected. |
| Vulture | Broad form remains, but feather groups merge, the eye/beak shift in color, and green/purple edging appears around body and legs. | Head/neck skin, the eye and feather interiors become unrelated scene-like patches. The remaining original feather edges and neck outline reveal the protected-mask geometry. Rejected. |

The direct encoder/decoder reconstruction is already unsuitable as a finished original: it loses high-frequency anatomy/material detail and introduces chromatic artifacts. The SDXS pass is a more severe failure, replacing interior identity rather than finishing texture. Similar brown/teal content across subjects is an observed symptom, not proof of a particular model or scheduler defect.

The four-pixel protected mask does its assigned job: source alpha, outside paint and ownership boundaries survive. Its success cannot establish preservation of eyes, markings, feathers, wing veins or interior anatomy. These rejected outputs are retained as the concrete control demonstrating that distinction. The conservation instrument and original result remain unchanged.

The separate phone probe preparation only binds a small-model transport/runtime experiment to the already precomputed embedding. It carries this visual rejection and does not establish physical-device memory, speed, compatibility, visual quality, library acceptance or production admission. No phone/device run was performed for this review.
