## Concrete RGB gate defect

`source/verify_gate.py:165` selects `masters/*/*.png` for `--all`, excluding `_captures` and `_calibration-builtin` but not `_banners`. `pixel_check` at line 78 converts a separate image to RGB, then calls `im.getchannel("A")` on the original image without checking its bands. An RGB banner therefore raises before it can receive a profile-specific diagnosis. The supplied review records exactly this RGB-banner failure. This assessment confirms the causal source path without rerunning it.

The smallest suitable CF design is registry/profile selection: opaque backgrounds are valid under an opaque-image check; cutouts must prove actual alpha and extraction quality. Blindly converting all inputs to RGBA would only manufacture opaque alpha and still misclassify backgrounds. Preserve malformed-image errors in the report. The current shallow glob also misses deeper profile/setting directories; a folder glob is not an authoritative expected-asset inventory. Its documented zero-attempt GO and tolerated block failure rate are operational library behavior, not sufficient acceptance of a required CF calibration set.

## Alpha and key-palette limits

The keyer first requires at least 92% of its approximately 3% border strips to match the key, or to be already transparent. It rejects other backgrounds. Alpha is estimated from color distance and magenta excess, then restricted near pixels classified as background; strong magenta pixels can become clear even inside an otherwise opaque subject. Spill correction subtracts red/blue on the rim, but **interior bleed and rose correction also change subject colors globally** (`source/chroma_key.py:164–215`). This is not merely edge cleanup. Intentional pink, violet membranes, colored glass, nebulae and emissions can lose color or coverage under these rules.

The gate's hot-magenta, green-rim, rose, clear-area and minimum-visible thresholds were calibrated for prior token masters. Some are absolute sampled-pixel counts and therefore do not transfer across dimensions or effects profiles unchanged. Existing alpha may pass extraction unchanged, but still needs independent halo, clipping and subject checks. Opaque backgrounds must bypass keying; glow/particles need a separate emissive workflow. Calibrate representative allowed colors, fine edges and intentional translucency over both light and dark backdrops, with explicit destruction/residue controls. If the subject cannot safely use magenta, choose and approve another extraction method rather than alter canonical colors to satisfy the old gate.
