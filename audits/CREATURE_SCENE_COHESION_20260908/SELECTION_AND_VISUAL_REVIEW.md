# Reference fit and scene review — 2026-09-08

**Decision: the selected Civet supports the approved Earth direction, but the full scene is not art-complete.** Root and an independent visual reviewer inspected the supplied Earth flora/fauna sheet, selected light/dark matte views, before/after 960×430 native scene, and root inspected the final phone page. No human acceptance is inferred.

The reference calls for natural named anatomy, whole silhouettes, quiet readable volumes and selective material detail: the eye, mask and muzzle lead; fur detail supports them. It does not ask for uniformly sharp highlights, fantasy anatomy or a fox replacement. Earth plants need their actual branching, crown, leaf, flower and fruit forms. Alien flora may use unusual coherent attached architectures under the existing seeded form/palette and biome rules. These are visual references, not instructions to replace identities or adopt their black sheet background.

## Selected and rejected images

The reference-led new generation (`civet-reference-refinement-original.png`, SHA c2ad0568182b7a4b60a9a569c65e6f8292e8a011209d1d5bcea6f4a37a3b4ece) was rejected. Its high-contrast fur competed with the face, and the generator again baked an opaque checkerboard. The single alpha extraction of it (`alpha-refined-v1`) lost substantial nose and dark tail areas. Preserve both source and failed matte; no production substitution.

The calmer original RGB from the first Civet packet is the selected base. User-authorized ImageMagick extraction repaired its nose alpha using the actual narrow source nose contour and luminance, preserving all 4,718,592 original RGB bytes. The resulting 768×512 lossless WebP is 179,816 bytes, SHA 186d76da888a4d6a1393eef85b0c47dfc3fd4f9653258dad3bdc027c4e400365. It retains the exact 29-field Civet identity and established pointed muzzle, rounded ears, tan spots, mask, four feet and ringed tail. Nose and tail are intact on both review backings.

**Remaining matte limits:** a thin pale fringe at back/belly/tail and fragmented fine whisker remnants remain visible on dark backing at large scale. They are less conspicuous in the small Earth placement, but production clean-edge acceptance remains open. Do not call the asset production-ready or regenerate all families from this study.

## Scene result

`native-ground-ruler/desktop-cohesion-before.png` and `desktop-cohesion-after.png` compare identical placement and background. A modest resident-only diffuse grade (brightness .90, saturation .90, tiny cool RGB balance, unchanged alpha) makes the coat less golden against the rainy background. Four tight Civet contact shadows plus weak ambient body shadow ground the feet; eleven measured supports add local contact to the existing five residents. The old residents already contain translucent painter ground shadows; their source bytes remain unchanged.

Civet stands at the wet mud/pebble margin above shallow foreground water, not on the raised far-right rock bank. No tall foreground occluder is justified at its current location. No grass, rocks, hard sun shadow, reflection, submersion, new species, or relocation is invented. The accepted relative anchors remain exact; they are not metre-scale ecology.

The scene change is deliberately modest. The independently reviewed comparison found no new clipping or distracting shadow blob. At 132px the silhouette, face direction, spots and tail still read; in the phone Earth inset the individual face is too small for a portrait-level expression claim. The five old flat residents—especially the Platypus and geometric plants—are the dominant remaining style mismatch. They require richer species-specific painting, not stronger filters. Civet close-edge cleanup and human visual approval remain next qualifications.

The one-view finite mesh and its fixed paws are unchanged. Full walking/jaw/flight/swimming, native game portrait/scene/battle integration, physical phone/Safari/PWA, native driver heap and heat, and full product admission remain unfinished. All earlier ROADMAP blockers remain binding.
