# Proposed compact-tail and occluded-ear representation — Dakk decision pending

Status: PROPOSAL ONLY. No intake, coverage threshold, family contract, anatomical
reference, motion limit or admission predicate changes in this batch. A short-tail
reference alone cannot repair a representation that demands four painted tail owners.

## Compact tail

Introduce an explicit `compact-observed-tail` representation, separate from biological
absence. The reviewer must identify a continuous, fully visible tail from its observed
root to its observed terminal tip in the exact keyed master. Bind the decision to
master, subject, prompt, declaration and reviewer evidence hashes. Retain all actual
tail pixels and the root/tip observations. Declare the exact represented chain and
unrepresented template subdivisions; never infer a missing subdivision from a low
paint percentage. A 0–12% tail3 score is evidence to review, not an exemption.

The contract compiler must route terminal-tail motion to the observed terminal owner,
without inventing a tail extension or silently dropping motion. Coverage then applies
to the explicitly represented chain and conservation still covers every source pixel.
All remaining anatomy, scale, contact, reconstruction, join and fold guards remain.
A biologically absent whole tail continues to require the existing separate review.

## Ear hidden by antler

Introduce `present-occluded` for the exact near or far pinna, with an identified antler
occluder and source-space evidence. It is not biological absence. A visible ear sliver
must keep its source ownership and observed attachment. A fully hidden ear may have
no painted owner, but cannot acquire a fabricated independent landmark or actuator.
The renderer must preserve the source occlusion; any pose that exposes missing paint
must refuse or require a newly authored source. Do not mirror the other ear silently.

## Required controls before activation

Positive fixtures: independently reviewed naturally compact tail with visible tip;
fully antler-occluded near ear; partially visible ear with all sliver pixels retained.
Each must run intake, family selection against held-out references, all actions,
composed native turns, full-size late-idle review and original-source conservation.
Keep ordinary long-tail and both-visible-ear fixtures unchanged.

Negative fixtures must reject: stale master/subject/prompt/declaration or review hash;
long tail erased or cropped to resemble short; tip outside frame or concealed; omitted
middle segment; lost tail pixels; fabricated root/tip; skipped terminal motion;
mandatory limb marked optional; whole-tail absence confused with compactness; ear
side swapped; no antler occluder; changed antler; visible ear discarded; mirrored
unseen coordinates; newly exposed blank ear area; unrelated missing anatomy covered
by a broad exemption. Mutate each field independently and require a specific refusal.

Recommended scope: implement these explicit representations and controls in the next
product batch only after Dakk approves this policy. Do not solve the seven holds by
lowering tail3 coverage or disabling missing-anatomy checks. Qualification/admission
remains per source; Elk/Moose and the five short-tail targets are still HOLD.
