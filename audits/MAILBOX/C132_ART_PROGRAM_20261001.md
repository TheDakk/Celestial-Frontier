- **Dakk's goal (2026-09-26, verbatim):** "the complete Earth creatures having full movement animations and all the procedurally
  generated animations in their various different battleground biomes." C15: every creature "properly painted and animated across the
  whole stage; and the 2D painted battle plays back and forth, completely smooth, with all its animations." Smooth 60 fps, never stepped.
- **Staging (ART_KIT Arena profile v4.2, MOTION_KIT §7):** side view, low eye level, fighters 1/3–1/2 frame height on the left/right
  thirds, ground line y = 0.78; three painted 2560×1440 plates FAR/MID/NEAR, parallax .10/.50/1.20 of the run-up; no camera moves.
- **Choreography (MOTION_KIT §5, ms × mass class 60–200 %):** timing bar → command → approach 420 → anticipation 140 → strike 90 +
  1-frame smear → hitstop 70×mass (≤ 140) + 2 white flash frames/120 fade + impact hold 240 + shake 6 px/180 + damage number
  (pop 90, rise 420, fade 160) → hit recoil 110/stagger 160/settle 180 or dodge 120/160 → return 380 → idle 2600–3400; cast
  rise 180/hold 120/release 90/settle 220; faint 520; victory 600. Anticipation, fast beat, overshoot, settle; heavy starts slow,
  light snaps; secondary motion by material (fur lag .08 s, chitin antenna quiver .04 s, slick 6 % squash, translucent wobble .12 s).
- **Attack animations (ATTACK_ANATOMY.md):** the attack comes from the creature's own painted part (jaw, claw, beak, tail, horn,
  stinger, pincer…): 14 families × 32 melee rows; the body supplies the motion, CombatCore's theme supplies the effect.
- **Attack effects (ART_KIT 4K, v4.3):** 11 ability themes, each a painted launch/travel/impact sequence (1024² cut-outs + anchor JSON),
  closed material table (theme hex is accent only); ≤ 3 textures + ≤ 200 particles per effect.
- **Arenas:** procedural and infinite per biome family, seeded from the battle context (never the clock); home-vs-visitor rule (wild and
  guardian fights on the creature's home ground, duels alternate). D3: roll out by biome family.
- **Sound (SOUND_KIT, approved paragraph):** per-theme cues, 16 battle cues, layered impacts (transient + mass thud + material tail),
  music ducks 25/90 ms, family voice sets.
- **Guardians:** painted at 1536², frame-filling, ground line 0.95; §20 parties of 3 for Guardians/Titans only, Auto or Command,
  identical rewards, defeat = Recovery, swapping never necessary.
- **Phone:** each rig update < 4 ms; every library pair 60 fps at 4× CPU (C15 bar); ≥ 30 fps on the physical iPhone with two
  fighters, one effect and three plates. D1: phones never run inference; they get delivered finished originals, painter fallback.
