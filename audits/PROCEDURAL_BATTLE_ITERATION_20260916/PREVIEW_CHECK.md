# Review player check — September16

The first gallery used four MP4 video elements. The embedded browser crashed while loading them;
no exact root cause is claimed. Replaced it with one player, explicit selection buttons and
preload=none. A fresh tab loaded its poster, showed the complete review copy, then played the
first ten-second film (pause icon,0:00/0:10 and an active time bar observed). No recovery loop
or change to encoded native evidence. The selected page was retained as a deliverable.

Native films were captured in isolated Edge, not this embedded player. Their performance numbers
are rig execution measurements, not a measure of the gallery or of physical iPhone execution.
