## Existing CF pipeline and the separate Blender experiment

1. Canonical seeds and existing domain owners produce the complete genome and descriptions.
2. Named/lineage or procedural routing selects the final morphology and deterministic painter;
   exact complete-genome identity binds caches and protected canonical portraits.
3. The new offline exporter evaluates fixed owned declarations and retains genome, visual key,
   final proportions, qualified lineage, derivation and source hashes. Its present Blender recipe
   admits only pure named Wolf. Real descendants and procedural controls keep static fallback.
4. The first connected Wolf master supplies authored depth, skeleton, weights and finite idle/
   inspection motion. Its structural PASS does not settle its clay-like material, abrupt joints
   or unfinished face/fur. The new coat/face and brace/neck-head strike/recoil study has separate
   render and motion PASS results but does not yet meet the requested visual quality. Native
   combat still uses its implemented whole-portrait translations.

## Current proposed painted workflow

The earlier concept → Blender geometry/material/rig sequence was a proposed CF authoring route,
not a description of Dakk's production pipeline. The supplied source supports a painted route:
canonical visual brief → reviewed space contract/reference → exact queued prompt → built-in
image-tool capture → retained original → profile-aware extraction/export → technical and visual
review → bounded browser integration. This route is proposed for CF, not already implemented.

The structured brief records exact identity and anatomy, environment/material cues, character,
palette, camera and viewing scale. Use the exact approved space and creature sheets as the
reference anchors for bounded asset work. Individual assets still require canonical identity,
anatomy and technical review; biome and UI references are forthcoming. Concepts do not rewrite
genes to fit a painting. Opaque backgrounds, isolated cutouts and emissive effects need distinct profiles.

Animation is additional authored work: compatible layers or geometry, connected anatomy,
joints/weights, hidden-part artwork, ground contact and finite clips need an explicit exporter
and browser presentation owner. The supplied image pipeline does not provide them. Blender can
remain an optional authoring route; a painting does not automatically become its rig. Choose the
bounded representation using one complete encounter and measured phone memory/loading behavior,
preserving complete-genome identity and canonical fallback. No runtime/dependency choice is made here.

Human review judges recognition, richness, charm and coherence across the creature and surrounding
UI. Only a reviewed bounded result proceeds to existing art/resource owners. Combat presentation
then consumes the actual settled transcript/cue clock for preparation, attack, reaction and
recovery, with reduced-motion/static counterparts, visibility cancellation and disposal. It
never creates gameplay outcomes. Broader family and universe coverage requires its own evidence.
