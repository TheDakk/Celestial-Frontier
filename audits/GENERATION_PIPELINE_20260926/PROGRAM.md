# The Generated Creature Pipeline (2026-09-26)

**Nick's decisions (D22, D23):**
- **Hybrid generation.** A large AI-generated library, made ahead of time and delivered on demand, plus the local finisher applied per individual on capable devices. Phones receive delivered finished originals.
- **Both lanes put the pipeline first.** Polish (audio Stage 4, the Kindred picker, extra parity) waits.

The vision, in Nick's words (2026-09-17): "AI generation must work for any combination." Every creature in the game shows its own AI-generated painting, animated by its own rig.

## Where we are (honest)

| Piece | State |
|---|---|
| AI painting of a creature | Works offline, by hand. Codex paints with the strong image model from the compiled Art Kit prompt (`tools/painted-creature/compile-master-prompt.mjs`), one generation per packet. 38 archetypes are shipped. |
| Painting → rig | **Manual.** Codex writes `authoring.json` by hand: pixel landmarks for every joint, a polygon per part, near/far layers, and the presence declaration. Then `tools/creature-animation/intake-authored.mjs` produces the fit. The intake compiler (`tools/anatomy-verify/`) finds landmarks for 5 of 7 subjects and does not emit part polygons. |
| Six marking masks | Generated by Codex per creature (image tool), with a conservation check. |
| Procedural creatures | Drawn as the nearest painted archetype, morphed by their genes. This is a stand-in, not their own art. |
| Local finisher | Desktop tool only (`finish-master.mjs`, 15–25 s per creature on the Mac). Not routed into the game. Phones not qualified. |
| Delivery | All art ships inside the 128 MiB pack (the arena is ~60 MiB). There is no on-demand art store. |

## Stages, owners, gates

**G1: Auto-author** (Claude builds; Codex reviews the contract).
- `painting + family → authoring.json` (landmarks, part polygons, layers), with presence taken from the verifier's visible-anatomy count, never from the template.
- The output goes into Codex's UNCHANGED `intake-authored.mjs` and fit chain.
- **Evaluation corpus:** the 38 hand-authored packets. Hand authoring is the reference, and the auto-authored fit must pass the same static and native gates.
- **Gate:** at least 30 of 38 admitted with zero hand edits, and a mutation battery that refuses (erased limb, duplicated limb, wrong template).
- Anything that fails is retained with its reason and falls back to the stand-in. There is never silent hand repair.

**G2: Library generation at scale** (Codex).
- Batch-generate from the compiler:
  - every Earth fauna species (~631) in its family's controlled layout (strict side profile, facing right);
  - plus N alien variants per body family, spanning the genome's visual axes.
- Each image passes the anatomy verifier (visible counts), then G1 auto-authoring, then the static and native gates.
- **Masks:** a procedural/derived mask path so six masks do not cost six image generations per creature (Codex's proposal, conservation gate unchanged).
- One review sheet per 50 for Nick (D1 cadence scaled).

**G3: On-demand art delivery** (Claude).
- A library manifest pinned with the C13 pin contract (per-file hashes, private frozen registry).
- Art is served from the dev/prod origin OUTSIDE the precached PWA pack, so the 128 MiB cap is unaffected.
- Fetched on first sight, admitted by pin before any decode, cached in a bounded store (quota plus LRU).
- Offline or refused → today's painted stand-in, labelled.
- A pack test proves the library never enters the pack.

**G4: Selection** (Claude).
- A creature's art resolves in this order:
  1. its exact species painting (Earth);
  2. the nearest library variant by visual genes (procedural);
  3. the stand-in.
- Its genes still morph palette, proportion and markings on top.
- Card and stage use one resolver (CARD = STAGE).

**G5: Finisher in the game** (Claude routes; Codex owns the engine).
- On capable devices, a discovered creature's texture gets the masked finisher once, as a background job on the landfall job queue, retained in `creature-originals`.
- Phones receive delivered finished originals (D1) and never run the model.
- Gates: `finish-conservation` (alpha, labels, binding equality), and a quality review by Nick.

## Order

1. **Now, in parallel:** G1 (Claude, on the 38-packet corpus); G3 (Claude); G2 pilot of one body family, ~20 Earth quadrupeds (Codex), feeding G1's evaluation.
2. **When G1 passes:** G2 at scale, then G4.
3. **G5** after G3 delivery exists.
4. Polish and QA after Nick's playtest (D21).
