# Centipede source and representation decision

Nick reopened the stopped Centipede item under ../AUTHORITY.md. The old two-span73-joint and one-span43-joint refusals remain in the signed repair packet; no old measurement or binding is relabelled. The generic reference entry remains Centipede with exactly its retained genome/visual key. The old generated source was described as scolopendrid, whose complete count could not be established from the image. A new source explicitly depicts an adult stone centipede with15 total pairs:14 walking pairs plus one ultimate pair. NHM describes adult stone centipedes as15 pairs: https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/how-to-make-pitfall-trap-to-catch-insects.html . The explicit ultimate-pair role follows the distinction reviewed at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6330759/ . This is an exemplar choice for the generic entry, not a taxonomic claim about the prior painting.

Each of28 walking legs keeps two real movable spans and its existing contact solver. Root/head/mandible/two antennae add5 joints; two ultimate appendages add2. The model therefore declares63 movable joints inside the unchanged64-joint cap. Body/head,28 whole walking limbs and2 ultimate limbs occupy32 real textures inside the unchanged32-part cap. Fixed source sockets locate the head and limbs on the rigid trunk; they have no pose channels, extra hidden joints or unconstrained motion. Each socket is admitted against exact source alpha.

The painted trunk plates do not articulate separately. The new opt-in myriapod-rigid-trunk-v1 model explicitly trades the legacy eight-segment wave for all observed appendages and their two-link walking articulation. Count-aware clips use level trunk motion, source steps, head/mandible/sensor gestures and alternating leg phases. All joint, bone, contact, compression, drift, mesh and acceptance numbers remain the existing limits. The model is not enabled for legacy no-count records. No accepted binding or S2 input is edited.

Whole-limb textures need multiple genuine influences. The packet compiler seeds manually authored proximal Knee and head appendage regions, runs the existing diffusion, restores inherited root/contact pins and validates the final field. UVs, source positions, topology, RGBA and solver profile remain unchanged. Regional tests must prove real paint movement and reject missing/conflicting influences; a list of positive weights alone is insufficient. Actual static/native acceptance remains a separate measured stage.

Candidate03 has independently inspected14 far and14 near walking chains,2 ultimate appendages and2 antennae. Parent presence is explicit. Internal centers and socket boundaries are observed authoring hypotheses, not image-derived anatomical certainty. The missed101px art margin and faint generated fringe remain visible, untrimmed, for Nick.
