# Far3 upper-shaft influence proposal

The direct source crop shows a continuous gold upper span from the authored Knee[835,592] down/right toward the source socket[851,629]. The existing16px Knee core describes its bend but does not explicitly describe that span. The brown dorsal trunk rim below/left remains a real fixed collar; it must not become a Knee region.

The two previously cited low-Knee-weight positions are limb fringe rather than opaque shaft: source[824,609] is RGBA[255,0,0,1], and nearby[828,614] is[170,85,85,3]. Opaque shaft examples include[839,602] alpha252,[844,612] alpha252,[849,614] alpha251 and[840,617] alpha251. This distinction matters: a full opaque-shaft seed can be anatomically grounded without pretending every surrounding mesh support itself is painted gold.

It selected11 existing fit08 field IDs. Three are the existing same-Knee core pins1581,1582,1583. Eight are unpinned:1584,1591,1614,1626,1627,1628,1634,1635. No selected support conflicts with an inherited root/contact lock. Full current/pre-regional weights and source hashes are retained in proposal.json.

The omitted outer fringe points are not snapped into the region. Their eventual response depends on the unchanged diffusion/field solve and must be measured later. No compiler, diffusion, skeleton, ARAP, contact replay, intake or qualification was executed by this proposal. No candidate11 or source authoring was written here.

Current compiler semantics preserve the intended constraint: pin:false seeds a soft weight, but it still refuses any differently owned inherited lock selected by the polygon. There is no silent lock skipping. Overlap with the already pinned same-Knee core is allowed and leaves those pins intact. A later compilation remains the authority; the source membership read is not an admission claim.

For the broader two-span model, explicit upper-shaft influences are more faithful than tiny bend cores as the sole evidence of upper-bone ownership. That does not justify an automatic nearest-bone rule, copied polygons, or an unmeasured all28 repair. Each ordinary limb has its own manually observed upper paint and collar. The retained complete remaining-failure union determines whether any additional upper declaration is needed for this item; it does not make a soft regional change itself a pass. Parent reports the bounded union remains far3-only, so a single far3 prototype is the currently supported scope.
