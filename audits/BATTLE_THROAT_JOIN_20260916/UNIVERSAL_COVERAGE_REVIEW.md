# Whole-creature coverage review — September 16

The requested invariant is continuous painted anatomy through motion for every generated or named creature, including land, air, aquatic and rooted life. A Civet fit is authored geometry, not a universal head placement. No Earth anatomy is overridden by genes.

## What this repair establishes

The previous body-preservation check could pass with an exposed notch between surviving body ink and a replacement head. The new `attachment-coverage.mjs` binds declared interior attachment points to actual triangles, follows their published vertices, and checks native rendered alpha between them. It has no species name, limb count or quadruped joint assumption. It never fills an inferred hole or closes intentional mouth, gill, fin or between-limb openings.

The Civet's declaration is hash-bound to its actual record and turnaround. Seventeen overlapping one-pixel-radius ribbons span the throat attachment. Their endpoints remain in source-painted head and neck surfaces. Rest fitting changes only the presentation alignment; original master bytes, bone curves, contacts, seed and kit text are unchanged. The existing source-edge checker independently covers visible original body joins on the Civet, fox and real procedural quadruped.

The fourteen inventories are quadruped, hopper, biped-bird, fish, insect, serpent, arachnid, radial, plant-woody, plant-herb, myriapod, cephalopod, flyer-membrane and primate. They are not complete coverage of every generator output. Existing observations already expose ten radial arms versus a six-arm motion template, variable myriapod segment/leg counts, and squid feeding tentacles absent from the eight-arm template. Specialist Earth painters, fungi, colonies and microbes also need actual ownership records. Their omission cannot be hidden by calling a calibration atlas a finished creature.

## Required promotion rule

For each authored or procedural instance: bind the winning painter's exact source hash, part labels, visible topology, material and attachment declarations; admit its matching family/count contract; preserve source rest pixels; scan the full action and aim envelope for orientation, shape, attachment coverage and contact; inspect real moving artwork and measure the complete runtime. Authored source views get their own fits; procedural views must come from the painter, not a Civet transform. Unknown or missing source coverage refuses articulated admission and preserves the original portrait as an explicitly labelled fallback.

The anatomical declaration must distinguish welded body skin from independent overlapping limbs and intentional openings. The existing quadruped source-edge classifier is still quadruped-specific; the new generic ribbon evaluator does not silently turn that classifier into a universal topology compiler. Completing painter-emitted attachment inventories and count-aware motion remains the next family work. Claude-owned modules remain untouched under the standing lane agreement.
