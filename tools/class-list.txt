FANTASY CREATURE CLASS LIST — Dakk, 2026-06-11 (verbatim source for the 1.0 class system; see ROADMAP "CLASS SYSTEM + XP/LEVELS")

Warrior — Weapon master creature with powerful melee attacks, heavy armor instincts, battle discipline, cleaving strikes, and battlefield dominance.
Fighter — Balanced combat creature skilled with many weapons, combos, parries, counters, armor-breaking attacks, and tactical pressure.
Barbarian — Rage-fueled creature with berserk strength, leap attacks, pain resistance, shockwave slams, and brutal close-range damage.
Berserker — Unstable fury creature that becomes stronger when injured, attacks faster at low health, ignores pain, and can enter a frenzy state.
Knight — Armored guardian creature with shield defense, mounted charge potential, taunts, defensive formations, and strong protection abilities.
Paladin — Holy warrior creature with divine smites, healing aura, holy shield, demon/undead damage, cleansing light, and resurrection powers.
Crusader — Heavy divine creature with blessed hammer attacks, sacred armor, holy bombardments, divine charge, and anti-corruption powers.
Templar — Elite holy defender creature with sanctuary fields, angelic protection, radiant spear attacks, oath magic, and guardian abilities.
Monk — Martial arts creature with high speed, chi energy, pressure-point strikes, dodging, stunning blows, and spirit-based attacks.
Mystic Monk — Enlightened creature with radiant chi, healing strikes, spirit palms, inner light aura, and calm-based power boosts.
Shadow Monk — Stealth martial creature with shadow clones, teleport kicks, silent movement, void palm, and darkness-based evasion.
Samurai — Precision sword creature with instant-draw strikes, perfect counters, honor stance, wind slashes, and duel execution abilities.
Ronin — Wandering sword creature with unpredictable blade techniques, dirty counters, survival instincts, and lone-wolf damage bonuses.
Duelist — Agile finesse creature with ripostes, dodges, disarms, critical thrusts, charm, and one-on-one combat mastery.
Swashbuckler — Flashy agile creature with acrobatic strikes, taunting, momentum attacks, evasive footwork, and stylish finishing moves.
Brawler — Brutish unarmed creature with grapples, throws, body slams, stun combos, hardened skin, and raw physical dominance.
Gladiator — Arena-born creature with crowd-fueled buffs, execution attacks, intimidation roars, weapon swapping, and champion stance.
Ranger — Wilderness hunter creature with tracking, bow attacks, traps, camouflage, beast bonds, and nature-enhanced strikes.
Hunter — Survival creature specialized in tracking prey, setting traps, exploiting weaknesses, ranged attacks, and monster-specific executions.
Archer — Precision ranged creature with piercing shots, arrow storms, ricochet attacks, eagle-eye aim, and elemental arrows.
Arcane Archer — Magical ranged creature with enchanted arrows, portal shots, explosive runes, seeking projectiles, and elemental volleys.
Scout — Fast exploration creature with ambush tactics, stealth movement, map awareness, evasion, and weakness marking.
Rogue — Stealth creature with backstabs, poison, lockpicking, traps, evasion, theft, and critical burst damage.
Thief — Sneaky utility creature with stealing, distraction, trap removal, escape skills, ambush attacks, and treasure detection.
Assassin — Lethal stealth creature with shadowstep, instant kills on weak targets, venom attacks, marked-for-death, and silent execution.
Ninja — Agile stealth creature with smoke bombs, shuriken, clones, wall movement, poison blades, and vanish abilities.
Shadowblade — Rogue-mage creature with shadow teleportation, cursed daggers, darkness clones, void slashes, and dimensional assassinations.
Demon Hunter — Anti-demon creature with dual crossbows or blades, traps, vengeance form, demon sense, and infernal resistance.
Monster Slayer — Creature trained to hunt supernatural beasts with oils, signs, traps, tracking, mutations, and trophy-based bonuses.
Bounty Hunter — Tracker creature with nets, capture tools, marks, traps, interrogation, and reward-scaling damage.
Dragon Slayer — Anti-dragon creature with fire resistance, scale-piercing attacks, wing clipping, dragon roar counters, and dragon-soul absorption.
Giant Slayer — Creature specialized in fighting huge enemies with climbing attacks, hamstring strikes, topple moves, armor breaking, and massive crits.
Undead Hunter — Anti-undead creature with holy weapons, grave seals, turn undead, silver traps, and phylactery destruction.
Mage Hunter — Anti-magic creature with silence, mana burn, spell interruption, anti-magic fields, and spell reflection.
Wizard — Arcane caster creature with broad spell knowledge, fireballs, shields, teleportation, summoning, time stop, and reality-warping magic.
Mage — General magic creature with elemental spells, arcane blasts, barriers, utility magic, teleportation, and spell amplification.
Sorcerer — Innate magic creature with raw spell power, overcharged casting, wild magic surges, elemental mutation, and mana explosions.
Warlock — Pact-bound creature with eldritch blasts, curses, soul drain, forbidden magic, patron powers, and dark transformations.
Witch — Curse-based creature with hexes, potions, familiars, dark rituals, charm magic, and misfortune manipulation.
Necromancer — Death-magic creature with skeleton summons, corpse explosion, bone armor, life drain, undead armies, and lich transformation.
Lich — Undead archmage creature with immortality, phylactery, soul magic, death beams, undead command, and forbidden spells.
Elementalist — Creature that controls fire, ice, lightning, earth, wind, and water through elemental storms, shields, avatars, and summons.
Pyromancer — Fire creature with flame blasts, burning aura, meteor attacks, lava waves, phoenix rebirth, and inferno fields.
Cryomancer — Ice creature with freezing blasts, frost armor, blizzards, ice prisons, shatter attacks, and frozen clones.
Stormcaller — Lightning and wind creature with chain lightning, thunderbolts, tornadoes, storm aura, sky strikes, and thunder god form.
Geomancer — Earth creature with stone armor, crystal spikes, seismic waves, walls, earthquakes, and mountain golem summons.
Hydromancer — Water creature with healing waters, tidal waves, water shields, drowning magic, mist form, and ocean summons.
Aeromancer — Wind creature with flight, slicing gusts, tornadoes, speed boosts, air shields, and pressure blasts.
Illusionist — Deception creature with invisibility, false armies, mirror images, mind mazes, fear visions, and perception rewriting.
Enchanter — Control creature with charms, sleep magic, weapon enchantments, mind domination, morale buffs, and enemy manipulation.
Chronomancer — Time creature with slow fields, haste, time stop, rewind damage, aging attacks, and future-sight counters.
Blood Mage — Sacrifice caster creature with blood bolts, blood shields, life-drain healing, crimson explosions, and blood puppetry.
Rune Mage — Symbol caster creature with magic runes, glyph traps, rune armor, spell seals, ancient inscriptions, and stored spells.
Astral Mage — Cosmic creature with starfall, gravity wells, comet strikes, astral projection, black hole magic, and celestial energy.
Voidwalker — Void creature with phase shifting, void rifts, matter erasure, anti-reality fields, and dimensional summons.
Dreamwalker — Dream creature with sleep magic, nightmare summons, dream prisons, illusions, lucid healing, and dream portals.
Fateweaver — Destiny creature with luck manipulation, rerolls, forced critical hits, curse of fate, death avoidance, and probability bending.
Psion — Psychic creature with telekinesis, mind crush, psychic shields, telepathy, astral projection, and mass confusion.
Cleric — Divine caster creature with healing, blessings, holy light, protection spells, undead banishment, and mass resurrection.
Priest — Support divine creature with healing prayers, shields, cleansing, morale buffs, sanctuary fields, and miracles.
Druid — Nature creature with shapeshifting, plant control, animal summoning, weather magic, healing, and primal avatar forms.
Shaman — Spirit creature with totems, ancestral spirits, healing rain, spirit animals, earthquakes, and elemental rituals.
Oracle — Prophecy creature with foresight, fate reading, divine warnings, unavoidable curses, hidden truth detection, and prediction.
Seer — Vision-based creature with enemy prediction, future sight, weakness reveal, illusion detection, and destiny warnings.
Inquisitor — Holy hunter creature with lie detection, anti-magic judgment, corruption purge, holy chains, and execution powers.
Exorcist — Spirit-banishing creature with sacred seals, possession cleansing, ghost damage, portal closing, and curse removal.
Witch Doctor — Spirit and poison creature with hexes, plague clouds, spirit swarms, voodoo dolls, zombie pets, and giant spirit summons.
Death Knight — Undead armored creature with frost aura, soul blade, plague attacks, undead summons, life drain, and unholy resurrection.
