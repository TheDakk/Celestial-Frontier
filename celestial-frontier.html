Celestial Frontier

You are not the first to chart this universe. An order of explorers came before you — the Pathfinders — and for an age their beacons mapped the dark. Then, one by one, the beacons went quiet. They died a single discovery short of completing their master survey.

What they left behind: nine unclaimed Signatures; field notes that swear the spectrum runs deeper than Prismatic; and scattered warnings of named titans that rule the worlds where it runs deepest. Document a worthy example of every Signature, and the completed Prime Codex will reveal what no chart has ever shown — the Celestial Frontier.

Master the infinite — you cannot finish it. But you can become the first Celestial Pathfinder.

First, what shall the stars call you?
