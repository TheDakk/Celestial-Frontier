# Batch C original audio pilot — rights and processing record

These eight sounds were authored for Celestial Frontier with REAPER 7.79 and Surge XT 1.3.4. The music is an original eight-bar phrase and harmony arrangement; the temperate ambience is seed-authored wind and leaf rustle. The UI, Scout and combat cues use original parameter design and arrangement. No external recordings, animal calls or third-party sample libraries were used. Canonical creature voices remain synthesized by the existing runtime.

The embedded Init Saw and Init Sine template metadata identifies the Surge Synth Team and CC0. The official [Surge FAQ](https://surge-synthesizer.github.io/faq/) was checked on 2026-09-05: it permits sharing, sale and commercial licensing of generated sounds, including sounds made from factory presets. Surge application-source licensing does not impose a license on these rendered sounds. This record documents the project's right to use, modify and redistribute its original game audio; it does not redistribute the Surge application or assign a new public license to the private editable sources.

The private composition and source package is identified as `cf-pilot-c-original-audio-20260905`. Its editable project, MIDI, XML patch state, seeded source-generation code, PCM24 masters and full logs remain outside the public repository. Only optimized PCM16 WAV outputs are shipped. Their exact file and master hashes, native loudness readings, decoded shape and processing summary are in the sibling JSON proof.

The first render completed but its post-render crop assertion failed: the project had loaded MIDI at 120 BPM instead of the authored 80 BPM. It remains failed evidence. The corrected session used the native tempo API and verified all 135 note starts/ends and four audio-item positions/lengths before saving and again after reopening. The saved project matched 80 BPM; the corrected render, exports and final-file measurements succeeded. Its process exited 0 in 11.506 seconds on 2026-09-06 UTC. No screen inspection or UI automation was used; prior device prompts mean unattended startup is not certified.

Production used 48 kHz PCM24 stereo rendering. Music phrase and harmony/pulse layers were cropped from settled middle cycles and combined, with circular reflection taps and 8 ms loop-boundary smoothing. The 24-second ambience uses periodic shaped noise. Short cues were cropped, folded to mono and faded. Native LUFS-I and true-peak measurements of the masters selected constant export gains, with a -6 dBTP target ceiling. PCM16 export applies deterministic TPDF dither. The final PCM16 files were then measured again with REAPER CalculateNormalization modes 0 (LUFS-I) and 3 (true peak). RMS and mono-fold statistics are separately labeled and are not substitutes for those measurements.

The explicit pilot intake policy allows only 48 kHz PCM16 WAV, durations from 100 to 24,000 ms, integrated loudness from -36 to -20 LUFS-I and true peak no higher than -3 dBTP. The outputs total 9,752,032 bytes; decoding all eight as float32 uses 19,503,360 bytes. These are this pilot's payload facts, not a measurement of a populated runtime cache or the complete installed asset pack.
