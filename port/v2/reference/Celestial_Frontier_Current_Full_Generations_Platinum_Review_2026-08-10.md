# 3. Sugar Glider and related gliding mammals

# 3.1 Sugar Glider — FIX

## What works

The current portrait includes several correct identity cues:

- very large black eyes;
- dark central facial stripe;
- visible gliding membrane;
- long furry tail;
- compact mammalian body.

## What is wrong

The animal is presented as a nearly symmetrical front-facing kite.

The membrane is too geometric and dominates the anatomy. The hands, feet, forelimbs, and hindlimbs are reduced to seam-like lines rather than visible limb anchors.

The tail hangs below the body and reads partly like a flat leaf or paddle. A Sugar Glider’s tail should be:

- cylindrical;
- furry;
- long;
- free of the membrane;
- trailing behind as a steering surface.

The current pose also hides:

- large rounded ears;
- grasping hands and feet;
- small possum-like torso;
- the wrist-to-ankle attachment of the patagium.

## Required correction

Use a three-quarter or side gliding pose.

Show:

- small possum-like torso;
- large hairless rounded ears;
- huge eyes;
- dark stripe from forehead to nose;
- forelimbs and hindlimbs at four clear membrane corners;
- patagium only from wrist to ankle;
- long bushy tail trailing independently behind the body.

Do not reuse the Colugo-style full diamond membrane.

---

# 3.2 Flying Squirrel — FIX

## What works

- rodent head;
- incisors;
- whiskers;
- large visible eye;
- long tail;
- visible loose membrane.

## What is wrong

The current membrane hangs under the body like a pouch or sling rather than being stretched between the wrists and ankles.

The animal does not read as actively gliding.

The tail is held upright and club-like rather than acting as a broad, flattened, feather-like rudder.

## Required correction

Use a clear gliding pose with:

- forelimbs extended forward/outward;
- hindlimbs extended backward/outward;
- membrane visibly stretched wrist-to-ankle;
- broad flattened tail trailing behind;
- enormous dark eyes;
- visible digits at the membrane corners.

The Flying Squirrel should remain recognizably squirrel-like and should not share the Sugar Glider’s facial stripe or the Colugo’s tail-enclosing membrane.

---

# 3.3 Colugo — POLISH / LIMITED FIX

## What works

The current portrait contains the correct high-level concept:

- very large forward eyes;
- mottled camouflage;
- membrane extending farther than the other gliders;
- membrane reaching toward the neck and tail.

That is the correct distinction from Sugar Glider and Flying Squirrel.

## What is wrong

The portrait is too geometrically diamond-shaped and reads like a kite.

The fingers, toes, and limbs are hidden inside seam lines.

The tail/membrane terminates in a sharp point rather than a believable furred tail enclosed by the broad patagium.

## Required correction

Retain the full membrane, but make it more organic.

Show:

- visible fingers and toes at the membrane edges;
- more horizontal or three-quarter body orientation;
- slightly irregular natural membrane contour;
- broad head and torso;
- tail clearly enclosed within the rear membrane;
- lichen-like mottled fur.

# 4. Why the three gliding mammals need separate scaffolds

These animals evolved gliding independently and should not use one shared membrane body.

| Creature | Correct membrane coverage | Distinctive tail | Primary identity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sugar Glider | wrist to ankle | long bushy tail, free of membrane | small possum, large ears, face stripe |
| Flying Squirrel | wrist to ankle | broad flattened feather-like tail | squirrel head/body, huge eyes |
| Colugo | neck to fingers, toes, and tail | tail enclosed by membrane | complete diamond-like patagium, mottled fur |
| Bat | elongated fingers to body/hindlimbs | varies by bat family | powered wing made from a hand |

The renderer should select one of these four distinct anatomical systems before color, markings, or pose are applied.

# 9. Platinum acceptance rules for lineage continuity

A lineage passes only when:

## High anchor — 0.90

- same Earth body/growth family;
- all signature anatomy retained;
- only light inherited variation.

## Mid-high anchor — 0.73

- Earth ancestry still obvious;
- one or two compatible alien traits visible;
- no family replacement.

## Mid anchor — 0.46

- visibly hybrid;
- altered silhouette allowed;
- locomotion and ancestry still traceable.

## Floor — 0.22

- strongly alien;
- retains at least one or two clear ancestral signatures;
- visibly different from 0.46;
- does not collapse into an unrelated generic family.

The visual changes should be monotonic rather than arbitrary.
