**Scope:** every image in the archive, reviewed at full 440×440 output size and through full-catalog contact sheets.  
**Review standard:** Earth organisms are judged against their real-world silhouette, body/growth plan and defining anatomy; procedural organisms are judged for coherent trait blending rather than Earth realism.

# Executive verdict

This is **the strongest complete species build reviewed so far**. The large systemic failures from earlier passes—wrong ownership counts, exact duplicate portraits, one-template fungi/microbes, and many obviously misrouted specialist animals—are largely resolved.

I would treat this as a **Gold Candidate**, but **not an unconditional biological Gold lock yet** if the goal remains that named Earth organisms should look convincingly like their real-life counterparts without needing the label.

The remaining problems are now concentrated rather than catalog-wide. The principal hold items are:

- the equid scaffold, especially Horse/Donkey/Wild Horse/Wild Ass/Wild Pony;
- a smaller group of mammals still carrying ungulate-like bodies;
- several small/medium cats whose coat is correct but anatomy is not;
- a few specialist birds and aquatic animals;
- a short list of plants with the wrong whole-plant growth form;
- one procedural phenotype that is too dark to read clearly.

**Do not run another global body pass.** The current build should be preserved and only the focused `FIX` list regenerated.

Several earlier problem organisms now have real, diagnostic silhouettes rather than generic family bodies. Strong examples include Angelfish, Barreleye, Basking Shark, Fiddler Crab, Flounder/Halibut, Flying Fish, Flying Gurnard, Gharial, Giant Anteater, Kiwi, Koala, Lamprey, Lionfish, Mudskipper, Nautilus, Nudibranch, Ocean Sunfish, Paddlefish, Pyrosome, Salp, Seahorse, Secretary Bird, Spoonbill, Tardigrade, Tripod Fish, Walrus and Whale Shark.

The canid group is also materially better than earlier builds: Wolf, foxes, dog-like forms and Maned Wolf now read far more like canids rather than deer with canine heads.

## Earth flora

A number of formerly incorrect iconic plants are now convincingly represented: Rafflesia, Pineapple, Cabbage, Carrot, Corn, Cotton, Joshua Tree, Rhubarb, Tobacco, Watermelon and Wild Strawberry. Carnivorous plants and several aquatic/flowering plants are also strong.

## Fungi

This category is very close to Gold. ## Microbes

No glaring blocker remains. The set now includes distinct amoeboid, diatom, dinoflagellate, Euglena, foraminiferan, Paramecium, radiolarian, cyanobacterial/filamentous and environmental bacterial forms.

## Procedural generation

The fauna, flora, fungi and microbe generators all show multiple structural families. The only material issue I found is one very dark fauna phenotype where the head/appendage attachments become hard to read.

# Glaring or high-value corrections before final biological lock

## Equids

The Horse remains the clearest example of the earlier global-pass problem. It still has a deer-like shallow body, nearly vertical legs, weak equine joints, no convincing mane, and an incorrect brush/radial tail. Donkey, Wild Horse, Wild Ass and Wild Pony inherit the same underlying problem.

Build **one correct equid scaffold first**, then derive the species:
- deep barrel and withers;
- sloped shoulder;
- muscular croup;
- wedge-shaped equine skull/muzzle;
- articulated knees/hocks/fetlocks/pasterns;
- hooves;
- mane;
- species-correct tail.

Zebra is the strongest current equid visually because the markings help, but it should ultimately sit on that corrected body too.

## Cats

This group is mixed. Lion and Tiger are much more convincing than the earliest versions, but several smaller cats still have hoof-like feet or an ungulate barrel. Cat, Bobcat, Caracal, Cheetah, Clouded Leopard, Fishing Cat, Lynx, Ocelot and Serval are the clearest cases.

Do not touch every cat globally. Build a correct feline base, then preserve each current pelt recipe.

## Specialist birds

Secretary Bird, Cassowary, Kiwi, penguins, waders and several other specialized birds now read well. The remaining obvious raptor problem is Eagle/Harpy Eagle: the current round side-bird body, small beak and weak/no talons do not communicate a powerful raptor.

## Specialist aquatic animals

Most of the former fish-body failures are fixed. The remaining obvious issues are concentrated:
- Deep Sea Octopus is still a one-eyed fantasy/dress-like form rather than an Earth octopus.
- Monkfish needs its enormous flattened head/mouth and lure.
- Moray Eel needs an eel jaw rather than a sucker-like face.
- Sailfish needs the huge dorsal sail to dominate.
- Mahi Mahi needs its steep forehead and long dorsal profile.

## Plants

The remaining plant problems are mostly whole-growth-form errors, not color problems. High-priority examples are Acai, Baobab, Bull Kelp, Cloudberry, Desert Rose, Devil’s Claw, Devil’s Club, Duckweed, Giant Kelp/Kelp, Miner’s Lettuce, Papyrus, Peanut, Pinyon Pine, Prickly Pear, Sargassum, Steppe Tulip, Sweet Potato and Yew.

A useful pipeline rule is to separate:
`source organism` from `harvest organ`.
Names such as Lotus Root, Orchid Pods, Pine Nuts, Spruce Tips and Barrel Cactus Fruit need an explicit metadata role so the renderer knows whether it is drawing the plant or the harvested item.
