## THE HARNESS — WHAT WAS FIXED, AND THE PART THAT IS STRUCTURAL

The code pass's verification never ran because its hunt→verdict join keyed on a
free-text `claim` the verifier rephrased. **This pass joins on `species` at every hop**:
each judge and verifier copies the identifier verbatim from its packet heading, and the
merge is a `Map.get(species)` in the orchestrator, not a text match.

Two further guards, because "join on an identifier" is a rule and rules get forgotten:
- **the expected-name set is checked per batch** — a judge's rows are filtered against
  the packet's own name list, so a hallucinated or rephrased species is dropped rather
  than silently entering the totals;
- **anything dropped is re-judged** in a mop-up phase, so a skipped organism cannot
  quietly shrink the denominator. 1,250 rows / 1,250 unique species, exactly.

**448 rows reached adversarial verification and 431 upheld** — a 3.8% refutation rate,
against 31% wrong *causes* in the first pass. The `verifyWhy` text routinely corrects
its own hunt-stage claim ("FAIL stands but the stated causes are largely wrong"),
which is the field to fix against. Never the `defect` line.
