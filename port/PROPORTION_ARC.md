---

## WHY THE EXISTING INSTRUMENTS CANNOT DO THIS

Every check the project has answers a question about ONE asset in isolation:

| instrument | question it answers |
|---|---|
| speciesaudit | did all 1,254 paint? any byte-duplicates? any clipping? |
| overridecheck | does every route key reach a real catalog species? |
| artaudit | are there dead painters, discarded rngs, degenerate salts? |
| proportioncheck (22a) | is the whole-subject aspect ratio plausible? |
| proportioncheck (22b) | does an end lobe out-mass the trunk? |

None of them knows **what the animal is supposed to look like**. They can prove a lizard
painted, is unique, fits the frame and is not absurdly elongated — and stay green while it
has a head twice the right size, invisible eyes, and the wrong number of legs. Wave 22b
proved exactly this: 22a reported CLEAN on the horned lizard Nick was looking at.

**The missing thing is a reference.** Not a threshold — a per-species statement of what the
real organism looks like, to compare the render against.

## THE STRUCTURE

### Stage 1 — BUILD THE REFERENCE ✔ COMPLETE (batch 45, 2026-08-02)
`port/v2/reference/` holds one row per organism, keyed by exact catalog name.

**Fauna** (631) — `aspect` (side-profile w/h), `headFrac` (head length as a fraction of body
length excluding tail), `eyes` (prominent | normal | small | hidden), `posture`, `mustRead`
(1–3 features without which the animal is unrecognisable), `note` (only where the species is
commonly drawn wrong).

**Flora** (334) — `form` (tree/shrub/herb/vine/succulent/…), `aspect`, `leaf` (shape and
arrangement), `leafColour`, `harvest` (the organ a player picks, or "none visible"),
`mustRead`, `note`.

**Fungi + microbe** (49) — `family` (the structural family), `aspect`, `colour`, `mustRead`,
`scale` (microbes: single cell / small colony / field of many), `note`.

Generated by parallel subagents, one per catalog slice, from real-world anatomy. Rows marked
`UNKNOWN - guessed from name` are flagged for human review rather than silently trusted.

**LANDED: 1,014 rows — fauna 631/631, flora 334/334, fungi+microbe 49/49, exact one-to-one with
the verbatim catalog, ZERO rows flagged UNKNOWN.** Gated by `tools/referencecheck.mjs`,
negative-controlled in both directions (five defect classes injected, all five caught; exit 1
broken, exit 0 restored).

⚠ The gate fired on ITSELF first: it rejected `headFrac: 0` on 23 rows — but 0 is the correct
answer for starfish, clams, corals, sponges, anemones and barnacles, which have no head. The
data was right and my range was wrong. Suspect the instrument first.

The `note` column is the highest-value field in the table: it names how each organism is
COMMONLY DRAWN WRONG. Already in the data:
- Turtle — "the shell IS the body wall; legs and neck emerge from under its rim, it is not a hat"
- Chanterelle — "blunt forking ridges, not blade gills; a vase profile, not a flat cap"
- Peanut — "pods form underground; never draw them hanging on the plant"

⚠ THE REFERENCE IS DATA, NOT TRUTH. It is a second opinion from the same kind of source that
draws the art. Where it disagrees with a render, look at BOTH before changing either — and
where Nick disagrees with the reference, Nick wins and the row gets corrected.

### Stage 2 — THE CONFORMANCE CHECK
Extend `tools/proportioncheck.mjs` into `tools/conformance.mjs`: render every species, measure
it, and diff against its reference row. Report per species:
- aspect delta beyond ±35%
- headFrac delta beyond ±40%
- `eyes: prominent` but no high-contrast blob in the head region
- `mustRead` features that the route table demonstrably cannot express (e.g. the species falls
  through to the verbatim engine, or its spec has no parameter for the named feature)

**Negative-control it in both directions before trusting it** (PROCESS_LAWS): break a known-good
species on purpose and confirm the check fails; fix it and confirm it passes.

### Stage 3 — THE CORRECTION WAVES
Work the conformance report in descending severity, in batches of ~40 species, each batch:
strip-verify → full gate battery → record → commit. Prefer teaching a shared system a new
parameter over forking a bespoke painter (D-ART-67); fork only where no parameter reaches the
signature.

### Stage 4 — RE-EXPORT AND RE-AUDIT
Re-run the five zips and hand back for the external Platinum audit.

## KNOWN OPEN DEFECTS ENTERING THIS ARC (do not lose these)

- **The quadruped torso.** Better after 22b (real waist, rounded chest) but the small
  carnivores — sand cat, caracal, possum, mongoose — still read as tubes with legs. The
  silhouette needs shoulder and haunch MASS, not just a belly line.
- **Big cats have no route.** Tiger and others still fall through to the verbatim engine.
  Lion was routed in 22b; the rest were not. *A missing route is invisible to overridecheck,
  which only proves that keys we DID write reach real species (D-ART-71).*
- **The end-lobe threshold needs recalibrating.** It went 19 → 42 in 22b because heads
  correctly got bigger. Until it is re-tuned against the new head rule it is not a gate.
- **Platypus** has no route; measured 3.35 aspect against a real ~2.6.
- **The 17 flora NEEDS_FIX rows** from the Platinum audit are still outstanding (task #24):
  Acai, Angel's Trumpet, Beach Morning Glory, Black Pepper, Bladderwrack, Buckwheat, Cardamom,
  Cinnamon, Cotton, Cucumber, Dragon Fruit, Ivy, Mustard, Peanut, Sargassum, Tea Tree.
  (Ice Algae is already handled by the wave-18 CANON map.)

## THE LAWS THIS ARC RUNS UNDER

Carried from DEVIATIONS.md, because every one of them was paid for:

- **D-ART-14** never override what already excels — default new parameters so existing good
  painters are byte-unchanged.
- **D-ART-67** teach the system before you fork it.
- **D-ART-68** scale is the signature — a feature the animal IS gets drawn at the size it is.
- **D-ART-69** a signature must wear the body's light and taper INTO the body.
- **D-ART-72** measure, don't eyeball; and print what the instrument excluded.
- **D-ART-75** a bounding box cannot see a part. When an instrument passes, ask what it is
  structurally incapable of seeing.
- **D-ART-76** a head belongs to LENGTH, not depth.
- **D-ART-77** a mane frames a face, it never fills it.
- **D-ART-78** spines are rooted in the surface they grow from and belong to the right part.
