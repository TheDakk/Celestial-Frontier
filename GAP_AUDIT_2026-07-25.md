# Celestial Frontier v1.7 — Build Gap Audit and Completion Plan

**Release:** V1.7  
**Status:** Design-complete, implementation-incomplete  
**Audience:** Simulation, procedural generation, economy, crafting, combat, UI, Codex, art, QA, engineering, production, and release teams  
**Purpose:** Identify missing systems, implementation contracts, numerical balance, technical risks, and release gates not fully covered by the current V1.7 materials, gear, rarity, modifier, and art specifications.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The current V1.7 documentation establishes a strong and coherent creative foundation for:

- Universal rarity
- Flora and fauna rarity resolution
- Planet and star modifiers
- Materials and ingredients
- Crafting and refinement
- Gear identity
- Unique designations
- Procedural art
- Planet, moon, creature, flora, and vista presentation
- Gold-readiness assessment

The project is **not missing another major creative pillar**.

The principal omissions are the implementation systems that convert the design into a release-ready build:

- Numerical balance tables
- Complete economy lifecycle
- Recipe and progression acquisition
- Gear-slot and affix architecture
- Modifier compatibility data
- Ecosystem population rules
- Current implementation-status tracking
- Save migration
- Performance limits
- Accessibility
- Technical art requirements
- Release ownership and measurable acceptance gates

### Overall decision

> **Celestial Frontier V1.7 is design-complete but not implementation-complete.**

The project should not reopen the core rarity, crafting, procedural-generation, or art direction. The next phase should focus on data contracts, tuning, integration, QA, and release control.

```text

```

This separation must be preserved during implementation.

## 2.3 Unique designation

Unique remains:

- A protected one-of-one designation
- Separate from rarity
- Supported by a stable `unique_key`
- Reserved for authored or explicitly protected entities

## 2.4 Procedural-generation direction

The current procedural model correctly establishes that:

- Worlds influence organism and resource eligibility
- Organisms resolve their own rarity
- Materials resolve their own rarity
- Gear resolves from its completed identity
- Modifiers must alter actual generation
- Dedicated deterministic hash streams must be used
- Landing performance must not rewrite the generated world

## 2.5 Art direction

The current art review correctly identifies:

- Procedural creatures as broadly blended and structurally approved
- Flora canopy construction as the primary unresolved visual system
- Planets as Near-Gold
- Moons as requiring surface-detail cleanup
- Live ring compositing as a Gold blocker
- Selected vista and proof-layout issues as remaining release work
