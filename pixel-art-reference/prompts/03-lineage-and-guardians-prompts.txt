CELESTIAL FRONTIER V2
Procedural fauna, breeding, and Guardian art references

DESIGN NOTES

Seeded fauna. The three silhouettes share a body family. Leg proportions, body depth, tail shape, armor profile, markings and palette can alter the read while compatible attachment and motion rules keep the family coherent. Choose representative extremes before producing a full set of parts. The sheet is illustrative.

Breeding. LINEAGE_AND_BREEDING.md describes the three origins, the dominant Earth scaffold and mate-driven changes to Earth anchor strength across generations. On success, v2 keeps the parents, enters them into Recovery and produces a deterministic child. Use the existing inherited genome and set-qualified lineage to select compatible visual traits. In the illustrative Wolf and Ridgeback sheet, the descendants retain a canid muzzle, torso and leg structure while changing plate coverage, ear profile, flank markings and tail bands. The Earth Cross tile is an illustrative Earth-heavy comparison. The crosses on the board are authored examples rather than outputs of known seeds.

Guardians. The Earth Guardian art follows the existing Brown Bear painting. Reef and Sky Guardians are speculative body-family treatments. The pale silhouettes in the study are staging cues rather than a size chart. A separate Guardian presentation can use pose, framing and impacts to convey weight, while keeping the anatomy readable. The iPhone screen illustrates the Command break at a phase change, with one active companion, two reserves, and Hold, Swap or Withdraw. The full HP bar in the art is placeholder UI. Actual health and phase values come from the game.

IMPLEMENTATION DIRECTION

1. Resolve the existing body family and owner from the complete genome and lineage. Authored native-grid assemblies provide silhouettes, visible inherited detail and matching motion poses.
2. Select morphology, palette, markings and compatible attachments through a deterministic, versioned ruleset that accepts the resolved traits. Reuse the same appearance recipe for battle, Compendium and Lineage. Draw per-family idle, approach, hit and recovery poses so anatomy and markings hold between frames.
3. Constrain what a given family can accept. Review extreme proportions, tail and armor joins, visibility at phone scale, reproducibility, and attribution of visible inherited traits.
4. Treat Guardian roles and encounter effects as a presentation layer of the applicable anatomy and gameplay identity. Use the actual relay, phase and health state for the controls and bars.
